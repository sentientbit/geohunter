## Full description

GeoHunter turns the world around you into a game board. Walk to real places — woodlines,
ruins, libraries, markets — to mine resources, gather materials, and forge equipment. The
ground under your feet is the controller.

**Explore to play.** Points of interest appear on a live map of where you actually are.
Visit them to mine, to gather, to discover. Day and night, your map changes with you.

**Forge and research.** Turn raw materials and blueprints into gear. Invest blueprints to
advance your research and unlock what you can craft next.

**The Keeper's Journal.** Some places remember what happened there. The Keeper keeps
fragments of lost accounts — and you recover them by walking. Each page you reach is a
piece of a story that only opens when your feet take you to the right kind of place. It is
not a quest log with checkboxes; it is an archive you reconstruct, step by step.

**Trade and band together.** Swap blueprints, join a guild, add friends, and test your
gear in battle.

GeoHunter is free to play. You'll need to allow location while playing, and the camera
only if you choose to add a landmark photo.

Published by Sentient Bit, Brașov, Romania.
