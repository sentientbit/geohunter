GeoHunter ("the app", "we", "us") is a location-based exploration game published by
Sentient Bit, Brașov, Romania. This policy explains what data the app collects, why,
and your choices. Contact: office@sentientbit.com.

## What we collect

**Location.** GeoHunter is a location-based game. With your permission, the app uses
your device's GPS location to show nearby points of interest, to register visits to
real-world locations, and to power gameplay (mining, exploration, the Keeper's Journal).
Location is used while you play; we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

**Camera & photos.** If you choose to add a landmark photo or set a profile picture,
the app accesses your camera or photo library for that action only. You are always the
one who initiates it.

**Account data.** When you create an account we store your username, email address, and
gameplay progress (level, inventory, coins, quests, and similar). Your password is never
stored in readable form.

**Technical data.** Basic data needed to run the service — your authentication token and
in-game state — is exchanged with our server at thegeohunter.com.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not use your location for advertising or tracking across other apps.
- We do not access your contacts, messages, browsing history, or files beyond a photo
  you explicitly choose to upload.

## How data is stored

Gameplay and account data is stored on our servers (thegeohunter.com). Your login token
is stored securely on your device. Communication with the server uses HTTPS.

## Your choices

- **Location** can be granted or denied at any time in your device settings. Denying it
  limits location-based features but you can still browse the app.
- **Camera/photos** access is only requested when you add a picture.
- **Account deletion** — to delete your account and associated data, contact
  office@sentientbit.com and we will remove it.

## Children

GeoHunter is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and
we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly. Continued use
after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
